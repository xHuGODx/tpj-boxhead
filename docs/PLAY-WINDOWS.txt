BOXHEAD - Windows edition

Extract the ZIP, then double-click Boxhead.exe.
No Python installation or separate assets folder is needed.

CONTROLS
WASD: move. SPACE: shoot in your movement/facing direction.
ESC: pause. F11: fullscreen. F3: debug hitboxes.
Menus: WASD to navigate, SPACE to confirm.
Map selection: A/D selects map; W/S selects your purchased weapon.
Augments: WASD + SPACE, 1/2/3, or click. Release SPACE after shooting
before confirming an augment.

The pistol is free. Unlock more weapons and permanent upgrades in the shop.
Choose ONE weapon before each run. You keep it for the whole run.

Progress saves locally in %USERPROFILE%\.boxhead\profile.json.
Your friend's progress starts fresh; this package contains no personal save.
The first launch may take a few seconds to unpack the bundled runtime.
